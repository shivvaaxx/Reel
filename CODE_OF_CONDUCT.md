# Code of Conduct

Reel is a small project, and everyone who takes part (in issues, pull requests, discussions or anywhere else the project lives) is expected to be kind and constructive.

## Expected

- Be respectful, including when you disagree. Critique the work, not the person.
- Assume good faith, and remember that many people here write in a second language.
- Welcome newcomers and help them find their way.
- Accept a maintainer's decision on scope gracefully, even when it isn't the one you wanted.

## Not acceptable

- Harassment, insults, or personal or political attacks.
- Sexualised language or imagery, or unwelcome attention of any kind.
- Discriminatory jokes or comments about anyone's identity or background.
- Publishing someone's private information without their permission.
- Posting links or requests aimed at downloading content you have no right to share.

## Reporting

If someone's behaviour concerns you, contact the maintainer through the email address on [their GitHub profile](https://github.com/shivvaaxx). Reports are kept confidential.

## Enforcement

The maintainer may remove, edit or reject comments, commits, issues and pull requests that break this code, and may block anyone who does so repeatedly or seriously. Usually that starts with a private warning.

This code is adapted in spirit from the [Contributor Covenant](https://www.contributor-covenant.org).
